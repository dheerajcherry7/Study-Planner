Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

Study Planner is designed to keep ordinary planner data on your device unless you choose to use Google Drive features.

1. Local data

Tasks, habits, notes, preferences and study history are stored in browser local storage on your device. Clearing browser data may delete this information. Export backups regularly.

2. Google sign-in and Drive

If you choose Google sign-in, the app requests Google account identity and Drive file access. Files you upload are stored in your own Google Drive. The app does not operate its own file server.

3. Data sharing

The app does not intentionally sell your planner data. Google services, hosting providers and browser features remain subject to their own privacy policies.

4. Cookies and storage

Browser local storage is used to remember planner data and your sign-in preference on this device. Google sign-in may use Google's own cookies and authentication storage.

5. Security

Browser storage is not encrypted by this app. Protect your device and Google account. Do not store passwords or highly sensitive information in notes.

6. Your choices

You can log out, remove files from Drive, export or delete local planner data, and revoke the app's Google access from your Google Account security settings.
